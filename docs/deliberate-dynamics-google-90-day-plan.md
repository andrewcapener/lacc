# Deliberate Dynamics — Google Shopping / Ads Proposal: First 90 Days

**Client:** Deliberate Dynamics (deliberatedynamics.com) — veteran-owned premium tactical/military gear · Milkneck client
**Prepared by:** Andrew Capener · July 2026 · **Status:** Proposal draft for Monday standup
**Context:** ~$8M/yr B2B through Shopify. Paid D2C is at absolute zero — no Google Ads, no Merchant Center history. Authorized Arc'teryx PRO (ex-LEAF) dealer; also carries Ops-Core, Crye Precision, Salomon Forces, Raven Concealment, plate carriers, MOLLE, apparel, footwear.

---

## 1. Objective

Stand up Google Shopping + Search from scratch and scale to a **profitable, repeatable D2C acquisition channel within 90 days**, without tripping Google's dangerous-products policies or the client's dealer agreements.

**Day-90 targets (base case):**

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Blended ROAS | 3.0–3.5x (learning months lower, exit run-rate 4x+) |
| Monthly D2C revenue from paid | $75–100k run-rate by month 3 |
| Merchant Center | Healthy, zero policy strikes, >90% of eligible SKUs approved |
| Secondary | B2B/agency quote-request leads tracked as conversions |

---

## 2. Why this account is a strong bet

- **High AOV** ($150 accessories → $700+ Gore-Tex shells, helmets $1k+). Even modest conversion rates clear typical tactical/outdoor Shopping CPCs ($0.80–$1.50).
- **Scarce supply**: one of a handful of authorized Arc'teryx PRO dealers. Query demand for "arcteryx leaf / arcteryx pro" has nowhere else to go — and they currently capture none of it via paid.
- **Proven demand**: $8M/yr B2B says the brand and catalog convert; D2C paid is pure incremental.

## 3. The two things that can kill this account (handle first)

1. **Google dangerous-products policy.** Tactical is a minefield: anything weapon-adjacent (holsters, weapon-mounted accessories, some armor) risks disapprovals or Merchant Center suspension. A new MC account with no history gets no benefit of the doubt. We launch with a **policy-triaged feed** — apparel/footwear/packs first — and only add higher-risk categories after the account has trust.
2. **Dealer agreement terms.** Arc'teryx PRO dealer agreements commonly restrict paid search on brand trademarks, marketplaces, and MAP. Crye is similarly protective. **Open question for client before launch** — this gates the brand-search campaign, not the Shopping launch.

**Feed triage (verify each tier against current Google policy at build time):**

| Tier | Categories | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Apparel, jackets, uniforms, footwear (Salomon Forces), packs, gloves, base layers | In feed at launch |
| Yellow | Helmets (Ops-Core), plate carriers, pouches, eye/ear pro | Add weeks 3–6 after account is stable; watch disapprovals |
| Red | Holsters (Raven), anything weapon-attached, gated PRO "Mission" SKUs (verified mil/LE only), made-to-order 6–10 wk lead-time items | Excluded from Shopping; long-lead items only if availability is set honestly |

## 4. 90-day roadmap

### Month 1 — Foundation + controlled launch (Weeks 1–4)
- **Weeks 1–2 (build):** Merchant Center + Google Ads accounts; Shopify Google & YouTube channel for feed; GA4 + enhanced conversions + consent mode v2; site policy prerequisites (returns, shipping, contact info visible — MC suspension bait if missing); feed optimization (titles = Brand + Model + Gen + Color/Size, GTINs, product categories); policy triage above; quote-request form tracked as secondary conversion.
- **Weeks 3–4 (launch):** **Standard Shopping** (not PMax) on green-tier feed for full query visibility and negative sculpting. One brand-defense search campaign *if dealer terms allow*. Manual/target-CPC to gather data. Build remarketing audiences from day one.

### Month 2 — Learn → structure (Weeks 5–8)
- Split Shopping by margin/brand (Arc'teryx vs. Salomon vs. house categories); query sculpting with negatives; add yellow-tier categories if account is clean.
- Launch non-brand search on high-intent model terms ("arcteryx alpha jacket gen 2", "salomon xa forces gtx", "ops-core fast sf").
- Move best sellers to tROAS once ~30–50 conv/mo. Start B2B search test: procurement-style queries (unit/agency buyers) → quote form.

### Month 3 — Scale (Weeks 9–13)
- Migrate proven segments to **PMax (feed-only)** alongside Standard Shopping; scale budgets into whatever holds target ROAS.
- Remarketing/Demand Gen test on high-AOV browsers (shells, helmets).
- Day-90 review: unit economics per category → month 4–6 scale plan.

## 5. Proposed budget (media spend)

| | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 | 90-day total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | $5k | $9k | $15k | **~$30k** |
| **Recommended** | $8k (~$250/day ramping) | $15k (~$500/day) | $27k (~$900/day) | **~$50k** |

**Recommended: ~$50k over 90 days.** Enough conversion volume to exit learning and reach smart bidding thresholds by month 2, small enough that month-1 tuition while the feed and account build trust is cheap. Scale gates are performance-based, not calendar-based: month 2+ budgets only step up if prior month clears the ROAS floor below.

## 6. Projections (recommended tier, base case)

Assumptions: blended CPC ~$1.20, CVR ramping 0.8% → 1.4%, AOV ~$350.

| | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spend | $8k | $15k | $27k |
| Orders | ~55 | ~145 | ~300 |
| Revenue | ~$19k | ~$51k | ~$105k |
| ROAS | ~2.4x | ~3.4x | ~3.9x |

90-day base case: **~$50k spend → ~$175k revenue (~3.5x blended)**. Bear case 2.0–2.5x (still likely above breakeven if product margin ≥ 40% — see open questions). Upside 4.5x+ given the near-monopoly position on Arc'teryx PRO queries.

**Kill/scale rules:** ROAS floor 2.0x at day 45 on green-tier Shopping → restructure before spending up. Any MC policy strike → pause affected categories same day, appeal, don't argue with a new account's standing.

## 7. Open questions for the client (need before launch)

1. **Product margin by brand** — dropship margins set the real ROAS floor; 3x ROAS at 20% margin loses money.
2. **Dealer agreement ad clauses** (Arc'teryx PRO, Crye): trademark bidding, MAP, channel restrictions?
3. **Gated SKU handling** — PRO Mission (verified mil/LE only) must be excluded or the purchase friction will crater Shopping CVR.
4. **Inventory/lead-time feed accuracy** for made-to-order items (6–10 weeks) — misstated availability is a suspension risk.
5. Shipping/returns policy pages up to Google spec? Who owns fixes — us or their dev?
6. Billing: client card direct in Google Ads, or on our MCC?
